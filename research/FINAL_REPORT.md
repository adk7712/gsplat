# Tri-plane attribute field with a plane CNN for static 3DGS: final report

MLDA × EEE, NTU. Branch `triplane-field` of `adk7712/gsplat`. All numbers below are computed from
the result JSONs committed under `research/results/` (tables generated by script, not typed by hand).

## TL;DR
- We added a tri-plane field (three 32×128×128 planes, product fusion, 2-layer MLP) that predicts a
  per-Gaussian **colour (SH-DC) residual**, optionally refining the stored planes with a light
  residual CNN (`plane + conv2(relu(conv1(plane)))`), on top of gsplat.
- **Dense views (NeRF-Synthetic, 100 train views, 8 scenes × 3 seeds):** no variant beats vanilla
  gsplat on average (all within 0.02 dB PSNR). The field adds ~60% training time.
- The **plane CNN** has real but opposite per-scene effects (consistent across 3 seeds): better on
  mic, hotdog, lego; worse on chair, ship, materials. These cancel out on average.
- An **opacity-gated** field (follow-up fix) removed the CNN's losses but also its gains: ≈ vanilla.
- **Sparse views (12 train views, 1 seed):** field without CNN ≈ vanilla (+0.03 dB); field + CNN is
  unstable (−0.45 dB mean, −3.09 dB on materials with visible artifacts).
- **Conclusion:** in the forms we tested, the colour-only tri-plane field, with or without the plane
  CNN, does not improve static 3DGS on NeRF-Synthetic, with either dense or 12-view training.

## Setup (verified)
- gsplat 1.6.0 (this fork), torch 2.11.0+cu128, CUDA 12.8, Python 3.13, torchmetrics 1.9.0
  (recorded in `research/results/gate_test/_pip_freeze.txt`), Kaggle Tesla T4.
- Protocol matches the 3DGS paper's NeRF-Synthetic setup: 100K random init points in [-1.3, 1.3]³,
  white background, 800×800, 200 test views, 30K iterations (checked against arXiv 2308.04079,
  Table 2, and the 3DGS code; details in `research/README.md`).

- Field details, regularisers (TV on post-CNN planes), optimiser and eval protocol:

## 2. Why the CNN helps some scenes and hurts others
Full analysis: `research/analysis/cnn_fluctuation_report.md`. Summary:
- In chair and ship, 95–97% of the CNN's extra error is in the empty background (a tinted smudge
  under the object), not on the object. "The CNN blurs fine detail" is rejected.
- Where the CNN helps, it roughly halves how much colour the field adds; where it hurts, it
  increases it (rank correlation −0.76 over 8 scenes, p = 0.04, ~8 tests run).
- **Unverified:** that the smudges are made of faint "ghost" Gaussians. The saved-Gaussian check
  could not see the region under the chair seat, because the visual hull most likely counts that
  hidden space as part of the object.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| triplane | +0.02 | +0.02 | +0.05 | -0.14 | -0.05 | +0.01 | -0.014 |
| triplane_cnn | +0.27 | +0.11 | +0.08 | -0.30 | -0.19 | -0.06 | -0.016 |
| triplane_gated | +0.12 | +0.02 | +0.02 | -0.14 | +0.07 | -0.01 | +0.013 |
| triplane_cnn_gated | +0.07 | +0.05 | +0.01 | -0.10 | +0.02 | -0.01 | +0.008 |

The gate scales the field's colour residual by each Gaussian's (detached) opacity. Because even
object Gaussians have median opacity ~0.2–0.3, it weakens the field almost everywhere rather than
only on faint ones. Re-running identical code and seed changed PSNR by up to 0.11 dB, so
single-run differences of ~0.1 dB are within noise.

## What was not tested (limits of these conclusions)
- **Only one seed for the gate test and the 12-view test.** Per-scene differences around 0.1 dB
  are within run-to-run noise there.
- **Not a reproduction of SplatFields (arXiv 2409.11211).** SplatFields predicts all splat
  attributes (not only colour), initialises inside the visual hull, uses a mask loss, and was
  evaluated at 4–12 views (it reports +0.51 dB over 3DGS at 12 views and +1.64 dB at 6 views,
  Table 2). Our 12-view numbers are **not comparable** to theirs (different views, init and losses).
- Not tested: opacity/geometry residuals from the field, multi-resolution planes, concat fusion,
  other TV weights or learning rates, 6 or fewer views, real-world scenes (Mip-NeRF 360 etc.).
- Triangle Splatting (planned Phase 2) was not started.
